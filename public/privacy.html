Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Flowza ("we", "us") is a customer messaging tool that lets businesses manage their Instagram direct messages in one inbox. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how you can have it deleted.

Data we collect

  • Account data: your name, email address, and a hashed password when you sign up for Flowza.
  • Instagram account data: when you connect an Instagram professional account through Meta's login, we receive the account's ID, username, and an access token. The token is stored encrypted.
  • Messages: direct messages sent to and from your connected Instagram account, and the sender's Instagram-scoped ID and username, so they appear in your Flowza inbox.
  • Files you upload: documents you add to your knowledge base, stored privately.

How we use data

  • To show and send Instagram messages on your behalf, only when you or your team (or automations you set up) do so.
  • To generate suggested replies with AI. Message text and your knowledge base may be sent to Google's Gemini API for this purpose.
  • To send account emails such as verification, password reset, and team invitations.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use Instagram data for advertising.

Who we share data with

We only share data with service providers that run Flowza: Meta (Instagram API), Google (Gemini AI), Amazon Web Services (file storage), and Resend (email delivery). Each one processes data only to provide its service to us.

Data retention

We keep your data while your account or Instagram connection is active. When you disconnect an Instagram account, we stop receiving its messages. When you request deletion, we remove your data as described below.

Data deletion

You can ask us to delete your data at any time:

  1. To stop access right away, disconnect the Instagram account in Flowza (Instagram Accounts → Disconnect). You can also remove Flowza in Instagram under Settings → Apps and websites.
  2. To delete all your data, email us at CONTACT_EMAIL from your account email with the subject "Delete my data". We delete your account, connected Instagram accounts, access tokens, messages, contacts, and uploaded files within 30 days and confirm by email.

Security

Access tokens are encrypted at rest, passwords are hashed, uploaded files are private, and all traffic uses HTTPS.
